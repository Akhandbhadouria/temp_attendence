# ClassAudit AI Institutional Attendance Policy
**Document ID:** POL-ATT-001  
**Version:** 3.2  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Last Reviewed:** April 1, 2026  
**Authority:** Office of the Principal  

---

## 1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

This policy governs the attendance and punctuality requirements for all teaching faculty members registered under the ClassAudit AI system. It applies to all full-time teachers, part-time instructors, and visiting faculty across all departments including Computer Science, Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, English, History, Geography, Economics, Commerce, Physical Education, and Arts.

---

## 2. ATTENDANCE REQUIREMENTS

### 2.1 Minimum Attendance Percentage
- All teaching faculty are required to maintain a **minimum attendance rate of 85%** across all scheduled working days in each calendar month.
- The working week is Monday to Saturday. Sundays are non-working days.
- A working day is any day the institution is officially open, as declared by the Principal's office.

### 2.2 Monthly Attendance Calculation
- **Attendance Rate (%) = (Days Present / Total Working Days) × 100**
- A teacher is marked "Present" only if they have successfully completed a **Face Recognition check-in** on the ClassAudit AI system before the daily cutoff time.
- A teacher is marked "Late" if they check in after the scheduled start time of their first class, with a grace period of **10 minutes**.
- A teacher marked "Absent" has not completed any face recognition check-in for that working day.

### 2.3 Attendance Thresholds

| Attendance Rate | Status | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| 95% and above | Excellent | No action required |
| 85% – 94% | Satisfactory | No action required |
| 75% – 84% | Warning Zone | Verbal warning issued |
| 65% – 74% | At Risk | Written warning issued |
| Below 65% | Defaulter | Formal disciplinary action |

---

## 3. LATE ARRIVAL RULES

### 3.1 Definition of Late Arrival
A teacher is classified as **Late** when their face recognition check-in timestamp exceeds their first scheduled class start time by more than 10 minutes.

### 3.2 Grace Period
- A **10-minute grace period** is applied to the first scheduled class time each day.
- Example: If a teacher's first class starts at 9:00 AM, they will be marked Late if they check in after 9:10 AM.
- If a teacher has no scheduled class for the day, the institutional cutoff time of **9:00 AM** applies.

### 3.3 Late Arrival Consequences

| Late Arrivals Per Month | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 – 2 times | No formal action (logged in system) |
| 3 – 4 times | Verbal warning from department head |
| 5 – 6 times | Written warning issued by Principal |
| 7 or more times | Equivalent to 1 absent day; salary deduction may apply |

### 3.4 Habitual Late Arrival
Any teacher who is consistently late for **3 consecutive months** will be referred to the Disciplinary Committee for formal review.

---

## 4. ABSENCE RULES AND PENALTIES

### 4.1 Absence Categories
- **Planned Absence:** Pre-approved leave (refer to Faculty Leave Policy POL-LVE-002).
- **Unplanned Absence:** Absence without prior approval or emergency leave application.
- **Medical Absence:** Supported by a valid medical certificate submitted within 48 hours.

### 4.2 Consecutive Absences
- A teacher absent for **3 or more consecutive working days** without approval will receive an immediate written warning.
- Absence for **5 or more consecutive working days** without communication will trigger a Show Cause notice.
- Absence for **10 or more consecutive working days** without valid reason may result in termination of service.

### 4.3 Unauthorized Absence Penalties

| Unauthorized Absent Days Per Month | Penalty |
|---|---|
| 1 day | No deduction if leave balance available |
| 2 – 3 days | 1 day salary deduction per day (LOP) |
| 4 – 5 days | 1.5x daily salary deduction per day |
| More than 5 days | Referred to Disciplinary Committee |

---

## 5. DEFAULTER DEFINITION AND POLICY

### 5.1 What is a Defaulter?
A teacher is classified as a **Defaulter** when any ONE of the following conditions is met in a given month:
1. Monthly attendance rate falls **below 65%**.
2. The teacher has accumulated **more than 7 late arrivals** in a month.
3. The teacher has **3 or more unauthorized absences** in any 30-day rolling period.
4. The teacher has failed to mark attendance via the face recognition system for **5 or more days** despite being physically present (as verified by other means).

### 5.2 Defaulter Reporting
- The system automatically generates a **Defaulter Report** at the end of each month. This report is accessible to the Principal via the "Export Defaulters" feature.
- The report includes teacher name, department, attendance %, late count, and absent count.
- A defaulter flag is attached to the teacher's profile, visible only to the Principal.

### 5.3 Consequences of Defaulter Status
1. **First Offense:** Written warning letter. Mandatory meeting with Head of Department.
2. **Second Consecutive Month:** Salary increment freeze for the current appraisal cycle.
3. **Third Consecutive Month:** Referral to Disciplinary Committee. A Show Cause notice is issued.
4. **Fourth or More:** May result in termination or non-renewal of contract.

### 5.4 Removal from Defaulter List
A teacher can be removed from the defaulter list by:
- Maintaining a minimum 85% attendance rate for **2 consecutive months**.
- Receiving a formal clearance from the Principal's office.

---

## 6. RECORD KEEPING

- All attendance records are stored digitally in the ClassAudit AI system.
- Records are backed up daily and retained for a minimum of **5 years**.
- Teachers can view their own attendance via the Teacher Portal under "Profile."
- Principals can view all attendance records via the Principal Dashboard and the "Reports" module.

---

## 7. APPEAL PROCESS

A teacher who believes their attendance record is inaccurate may:
1. Raise a grievance via the Head of Department within **5 working days** of the discrepancy.
2. The Principal reviews the system logs and face recognition data.
3. A correction, if approved, is applied within **3 working days**.

---

## 8. POLICY AMENDMENTS

This policy is reviewed annually. Amendments require approval from the Principal and are communicated to all staff via the institutional notice board and digital portal.
