## 1. GETTING STARTED WITH CLASSAUDIT AI

Welcome to the ClassAudit AI Teacher Portal. This guide will walk you through every feature you need — from logging in to managing your classes.

---

## 2. HOW TO LOG IN

### 2.1 Login Methods Available
There are two ways to log in to the Teacher Portal:
1. **Face Recognition Login** (Recommended)
2. **Password Login**

### 2.2 Step-by-Step: Face Recognition Login
1. Open your browser and go to the ClassAudit AI home page.
2. Click the **"Teacher Login"** button on the homepage.
3. On the login page, enter your **Username** in the field provided.
4. Click **"Start Camera"**. Your webcam will activate.
5. Position your face in the center of the camera frame. Ensure:
   - Your face is well-lit (avoid strong backlight).
   - Only your face is visible in the frame (no other people behind you).
   - Remove sunglasses or hats.
6. Click **"Capture & Login"**.
7. If your face is recognized, you will be automatically redirected to your **Teacher Dashboard**.

### 2.3 Step-by-Step: Password Login
1. Go to the ClassAudit AI home page.
2. Click **"Teacher Login (Password)"**.
3. Enter your registered **Username** and **Password**.
4. Click **"Login"**.
5. You will be redirected to your Teacher Dashboard.

### 2.4 Forgot Password
- If you have forgotten your password, contact your **Principal or System Administrator**.
- Password resets must be handled at the admin level via the Principal Portal.

## 3. HOW TO MARK ATTENDANCE

**Attendance must be marked once per working day, before your first class begins.**

### 3.1 Step-by-Step: Mark Daily Attendance
1. Log in to the **Teacher Portal**.
2. On your **Teacher Dashboard**, locate the **"Mark Attendance"** card or click **"Mark Attendance"** from the side navigation.
3. The page will activate your webcam automatically.
4. Ensure your face is clearly visible and centered in the camera frame.
5. Click the **"Mark Attendance"** button.
6. Wait for the system to process your face (this takes 2–5 seconds).
7. If successful, a green **"Attendance Marked Successfully"** message will appear.
8. If it fails, see Section 7 — Troubleshooting.

### 3.2 Important Notes
- Attendance can only be marked **once per day**. If you attempt to mark it again, the system will display: *"Attendance already marked for today."*
- If you missed marking attendance due to a system error, contact the Principal immediately with evidence.
- The attendance timestamp is recorded in the system using the current server time (IST — Asia/Kolkata).

## 4. HOW TO START A CLASS

**You can only start a class during its scheduled time window.**

### 4.1 Step-by-Step: Start a Scheduled Class
1. Log in to the **Teacher Portal**.
2. On your **Dashboard**, scroll to the **"Today's Schedule"** section.
3. Find the class you wish to start. A **"Start Class"** button will appear next to each scheduled class.
4. The **"Start Class"** button is only active:
   - On the correct scheduled **day** (e.g., the button for a Monday class only appears on Monday).
   - Within the scheduled **time window** (between the class start time and end time).
5. Click **"Start Class"**.
6. You will be automatically redirected to the **Live Class Monitoring** page.
7. Your webcam will activate, and face verification will begin every 5 seconds.

### 4.2 Starting an Extra/Unscheduled Class
- If you wish to conduct an extra class that is not on your regular timetable, contact the Principal to schedule a one-time session.
- Extra classes that are not in the system cannot be tracked through the Live Monitoring feature.

## 5. HOW TO RESUME A CLASS (REJOIN MONITORING)

If you were disconnected from the monitoring page (e.g., browser closed, internet interrupted), follow these steps to resume:

### 5.1 Step-by-Step: Rejoin Live Monitoring
1. Log in to the **Teacher Portal**.
2. On your **Dashboard**, you will see a banner: **"You have an ongoing class session. Rejoin Monitoring."**
3. Click the **"Rejoin"** or **"Resume Monitoring"** button.
4. You will be taken back to the **Live Monitoring** page.
5. The system will resume tracking your active presence from where it left off.
6. The `Total Active Duration` counter will continue accumulating.

### 5.2 How to End a Class
1. On the **Live Monitoring** page, click the **"End Class"** button in the bottom-right area.
2. Confirm the action in the popup dialog.
3. The session will be marked **Completed** and saved.
4. You will be redirected back to the Teacher Dashboard.

## 6. VIEWING YOUR RECORDS AND PROFILE

### 6.1 Teacher Profile
- Go to **Side Navigation > Profile**.
- Your Profile shows:
  - Personal information (Name, Department).
  - Monthly stats: Total Present, Late Count, Absent Count, Attendance Rate.
  - Class history with filter by date.

### 6.2 Previous Class Records
- Go to **Side Navigation > Previous Records**.
- Displays a full history of all completed class sessions.
- Each record shows: Subject, Date, Start Time, End Time, Active Duration.

## 7. TROUBLESHOOTING

### Issue 1: "No Face Detected" Error
**Possible Causes:**
- Low lighting or strong backlight.
- Face not centered in the camera frame.
- Camera not activated or blocked.

**Solution:**
- Ensure you are in a well-lit area with light falling on your face.
- Move your face closer to the camera if necessary.
- Check that no browser extension is blocking your camera.
- Refresh the page and try again.

### Issue 2: "Face Recognition Failed" Error
**Possible Causes:**
- A significant change in appearance since registration (new beard, glasses, hairstyle change).
- The face is partially obscured (mask, hat, hand).
- Camera angle is too extreme.

**Solution:**
- Remove any accessories and face the camera directly.
- Use the **Password Login** as a fallback.
- If the issue is consistent, contact the Principal to **re-register your face embeddings**.

### Issue 3: "Camera Not Found" Error
**Possible Causes:**
- The browser does not have permission to access the camera.
- No webcam is connected to the device.

**Solution:**
1. Click the camera icon in your browser's address bar and allow camera access.
2. Refresh the page.
3. If using Chrome: Go to **Settings > Privacy & Security > Camera** and ensure the site is allowed.
4. If no camera is detected, connect an external webcam.

### Issue 4: "Attendance Already Marked" Message
This is not an error. It means your attendance for today has already been recorded. No further action is needed.

### Issue 5: "Start Class" Button Not Visible
**Possible Causes:**
- It is not the scheduled day for that class.
- The current time is outside the class time window.

**Solution:**
- Verify today's date and the class schedule in the "Today's Schedule" section.
- Classes can only be started during their scheduled time window.

### Issue 6: Session Lost / Monitoring Page Shows Blank
**Solution:**
- Navigate back to the Dashboard.
- If an ongoing session exists, use the **Rejoin** button.
- If the session is missing, contact the Principal to check session status.

### Issue 7: Cannot Log In (Face or Password)
**Solution:**
- Ensure your **Username** is correctly spelled.
- Try password login as a fallback.
- If both methods fail, your account may be temporarily locked — contact the Principal or IT support.
