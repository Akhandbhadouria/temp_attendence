## 1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

This policy defines the types of leave available to all teaching staff, the process for applying and approving leave, consequences for misuse, and rules for unpaid leave. It must be read in conjunction with the Attendance Policy (POL-ATT-001).

---

## 2. TYPES OF LEAVE

### 2.1 Casual Leave (CL)
- **Entitlement:** 12 days per calendar year (1 day per month).
- **Purpose:** Personal matters, short errands, minor illness.
- **Advance Notice:** Minimum 24 hours prior notice required (except sudden illness).
- **Maximum Consecutive Days:** 3 days. Beyond this, a Sick Leave application must be filed.
- **Carry Forward:** Casual Leave cannot be carried forward to the next year. Unused CL is lapsed.

### 2.2 Sick Leave (SL)
- **Entitlement:** 10 days per calendar year.
- **Purpose:** Medical illness, hospitalization, medical procedures.
- **Documentation:** A valid medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner must be submitted within **48 hours** of returning to duty.
- **Maximum Consecutive Days:** 10 days on one application. Extended illness requires re-application and medical board approval.
- **Carry Forward:** Up to 5 days of unused Sick Leave can be carried forward each year, up to a maximum accumulation of 30 days.

### 2.3 Earned Leave (EL) / Privilege Leave (PL)
- **Entitlement:** 15 days per year, accrued at 1.25 days per month.
- **Purpose:** Vacation, personal trips, planned long absence.
- **Advance Notice:** Minimum **7 days** advance notice required.
- **Maximum Consecutive Days:** 15 days per single application.
- **Carry Forward:** Up to 30 days can be carried forward. Excess days are encashed at the end of the year.
- **Approval:** Must be approved by the Head of Department and confirmed by the Principal.

### 2.4 Maternity Leave
- **Entitlement:** 26 weeks (182 days) for the first two children.
- **Eligibility:** Female teaching staff who have worked at the institution for at least 80 days in the preceding 12 months.
- **Documentation:** Medical certificate confirming pregnancy/delivery.
- **Pay Status:** Full pay during the entire maternity leave period.
- **Process:** Application must be submitted to HR at least 4 weeks before the expected delivery date.

### 2.5 Paternity Leave
- **Entitlement:** 15 calendar days.
- **Eligibility:** Male staff for the birth of their child, applicable for up to 2 children.
- **Timing:** Must be taken within 6 months of the child's birth.

### 2.6 Compensatory Off (Comp-Off)
- **Entitlement:** 1 day off for every sanctioned working day worked on a holiday (e.g., exam duty on a Sunday).
- **Validity:** Must be availed within 60 days of the compensatory working day.
- **Approval:** Pre-approved by the Principal's office.

### 2.7 Special Leave
- **Occasions:** Bereavement (death of immediate family member: 3 days), marriage (5 days, once in service), blood donation (half day).
- **Documentation:** Relevant supporting documents required.

## 3. EMERGENCY LEAVE

### 3.1 Definition
Emergency Leave is granted for sudden, unforeseen circumstances that require immediate personal attention: hospitalization of a family member, accident, natural disaster, death in the immediate family.

### 3.2 Application Process
1. **Step 1:** The teacher must notify their Head of Department via phone call or message within **2 hours** of the emergency arising.
2. **Step 2:** A written Emergency Leave application must be submitted via the institutional portal or in person within **24 hours** of the first day of absence.
3. **Step 3:** Supporting documentation (medical records, death certificate, FIR copy, etc.) must be submitted within **72 hours** of returning to duty.

### 3.3 Emergency Leave Entitlement
- Emergency Leave is drawn from the teacher's existing Casual Leave (CL) or Sick Leave (SL) balance.
- If no balance remains, it will be treated as Leave Without Pay (LWP).
- Maximum **5 days** can be classified as Emergency Leave per year.

### 4.1 Approval Authorities

| Leave Type | First Approver | Final Authority |
|---|---|---|
| Casual Leave (1-2 days) | Head of Department | Head of Department |
| Casual Leave (3 days) | Head of Department | Principal |
| Sick Leave | Head of Department | Principal |
| Earned Leave | Head of Department | Principal |
| Emergency Leave | Head of Department | Principal |
| Maternity/Paternity | HR Department | Principal |

### 4.2 Rejection of Leave
Leave can be rejected if:
- Insufficient leave balance.
- The request creates a critical staffing shortage.
- No substitute arrangement has been made for the teacher's classes.
- The request is made outside the stipulated advance notice period without a valid emergency reason.

## 5. UNPAID LEAVE (LEAVE WITHOUT PAY — LWP)

### 5.1 When Does LWP Apply?
LWP is applied when:
- A teacher's leave balance (CL, SL, EL) is exhausted and they are still absent.
- Leave taken without prior approval and where emergency exemption is not granted.
- Absence that exceeds the maximum permissible duration of any approved leave type.

### 5.2 LWP Calculation
- **Daily Salary Deduction = (Gross Monthly Salary / Total Working Days in Month)**
- Each LWP day results in one day's salary being deducted from the monthly payroll.

### 5.3 LWP Limits
- A maximum of **10 days of LWP** is permitted in a calendar year.
- Exceeding the LWP limit will trigger a mandatory review by the Principal and HR.

## 6. REPEATED LEAVE CONSEQUENCES

### 6.1 Patterns of Concern
The following patterns are flagged automatically by the system:
- Consecutive Mondays or Fridays (leave sandwiching holidays) — **3 occurrences** triggers a review.
- Leave applied on the day of a scheduled exam or critical institutional event.
- Any absence immediately after a salary disbursement date, if it forms a pattern over 3 months.

### 6.2 Disciplinary Escalation for Repeated Leave

| Pattern | Action |
|---|---|
| 3 occurrences of suspicious leave pattern | Called for meeting with Head of Department |
| 5 occurrences in a term | Written warning from Principal |
| Exhausting full leave quota by August | Placed on a "Leave Watch" notice |
| LWP exceeding 10 days in a year | Referral to Disciplinary Committee |
| Fraudulent leave documentation (e.g., fake medical certificate) | Immediate termination |

### 6.3 Leave Watch
A teacher placed on a **Leave Watch** must:
- Submit prior written approval for all future leaves.
- Provide documentation for all future sick leaves regardless of duration.
- Check in with the Head of Department daily via the ClassAudit system for a period of 3 months.

## 7. LEAVE RECORDS & BALANCE VIEWING

- Teachers can view their current leave balance under **Teacher Portal > Profile > Leave Balance**.
- Leave history is accessible under **Teacher Portal > Profile > Attendance History**.
- Principals can view department-wide leave data in the **Principal Portal > Analysis** section.
