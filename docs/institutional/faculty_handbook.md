## 1. INTRODUCTION

Welcome to the institution. This Faculty Handbook outlines the professional standards, responsibilities, and expectations for all teaching staff. Adherence to this handbook is mandatory and forms part of your employment agreement.

---

## 2. CLASSROOM RESPONSIBILITIES

### 2.1 Core Teaching Duties
Every teacher is expected to:
- Arrive in the classroom **at least 5 minutes before** the scheduled class start time.
- Deliver all content as per the approved lesson plan and curriculum.
- Maintain an up-to-date attendance log for students at the beginning of every class.
- Conduct periodic assessments and submit marks within the designated deadline communicated by the academic coordinator.
- Cover the full syllabus within the stipulated academic calendar.
- Coordinate with the Head of Department for any changes to curriculum or assessment schedules.

### 2.2 Class Preparation
- Teachers must prepare lecture notes, presentations, or activity plans before each class session.
- All teaching materials should align with the prescribed textbooks and reference materials approved by the academic board.
- Advanced preparation for practicals, laboratory sessions, and field trips is mandatory at least **3 working days** in advance.

### 2.3 Student Engagement
- Teachers are responsible for creating an inclusive, safe, and stimulating classroom environment.
- Questions from students must be addressed respectfully, either during class or during designated consultation hours.
- Teachers are expected to hold a minimum of **2 office/consultation hours per week**, accessible to all students.

### 2.4 Assessment and Grading
- All assessments must be graded within **7 working days** of submission.
- Grade sheets must be submitted to the academic office within **48 hours** of completion.
- Teachers must not reveal results to students before official declaration.

## 3. PUNCTUALITY RULES

### 3.1 Institutional Punctuality Standard
Punctuality is a non-negotiable professional standard at this institution. Being punctual demonstrates respect for students, colleagues, and the institution.

### 3.2 Class Punctuality
- A class is considered to have **started on time** if the teacher is physically present and instruction has begun within **5 minutes** of the scheduled start time.
- Habitual tardiness (being late to class 5 or more times in a month) will result in a formal warning.

### 3.3 Meeting and Event Punctuality
- All teachers must arrive on time for staff meetings, department meetings, parent-teacher conferences, examination duty, and institutional events.
- Tardiness to mandatory meetings is logged and is subject to the same disciplinary process as classroom tardiness.

### 3.4 Face Recognition Attendance
- Daily check-in via the **ClassAudit AI face recognition system** is mandatory.
- This must be completed **before the teacher's first scheduled class** of the day.
- A teacher who fails to check in faces the consequences outlined in the Attendance Policy (POL-ATT-001).

## 4. CLASS DISCIPLINE STANDARDS

### 4.1 Maintaining Order
- Teachers are the primary authority within the classroom. They are responsible for maintaining an orderly and focused environment.
- Disruptive behavior by students must be addressed immediately using institutional disciplinary protocols.
- Physical discipline of any form is strictly prohibited. Any violation of this rule will result in immediate termination.

### 4.2 Mobile Phone Policy
- Teachers should refrain from personal mobile phone use during class hours, except in designated emergencies.
- Personal calls during class must be taken outside the classroom only if absolutely necessary, and must be kept brief (under 2 minutes).

### 4.3 Classroom Environment
- Classrooms must be kept clean and organized. Teachers are responsible for the state of the room during their session.
- All audio-visual equipment (projectors, computers, speakers) must be switched off and secured at the end of each class.

### 4.4 Student Data Privacy
- Teachers must not share individually identifiable student performance data with other students or parents without authorization.
- All student-related documents must be stored securely, whether physical or digital.

## 5. PROFESSIONAL BEHAVIOR EXPECTATIONS

### 5.1 Code of Conduct
All teaching staff are expected to:
- Conduct themselves with **integrity, respect, and professionalism** at all times on institutional premises and in all institutional communications.
- Treat all students, staff, parents, and visitors with fairness, dignity, and courtesy regardless of gender, religion, caste, nationality, or background.
- Refrain from any form of **harassment, bullying, or discrimination**.
- Uphold the institution's academic integrity standards — plagiarism, falsification of records, or fraudulent reporting is grounds for immediate termination.

### 5.2 Conflict of Interest
- Teachers must disclose to the Principal any personal relationships with students (e.g., tutoring for pay, familial relationship) that could create a conflict of interest.
- Commercial tutoring of the institution's own students without prior written approval from the Principal is prohibited.

### 5.3 Social Media and Public Communication
- Teachers must not make public statements on social media platforms that reflect negatively on the institution, its students, or staff.
- Sharing student images, videos, or data on any public platform without explicit written consent from the student/parent is a violation of privacy law and institutional policy.

### 5.4 Dress Code
- Teaching staff are expected to dress in professional, formal attire appropriate to their role.
- Jeans, sportswear, and casual T-shirts are not permitted during teaching hours. Exceptions may be granted on designated informal event days.

### 5.5 Reporting Obligations
- Any teacher who becomes aware of a safeguarding concern, child abuse allegation, or safety hazard on premises must report it immediately to the Head of Department or Principal.
- Failure to report known safeguarding concerns is a serious disciplinary offense.

### 5.6 Intellectual Property
- All teaching materials, course content, and assessments created by a teacher while employed at the institution are the intellectual property of the institution.

---

## 6. PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPMENT

- Teachers are required to attend a minimum of **2 professional development workshops or training sessions** per year.
- Attendance at departmental training and workshops is mandatory unless prior leave is approved.
- Teachers are encouraged to pursue relevant certifications, higher education, and research — the institution may provide funding subject to application and approval.

---

## 7. GRIEVANCE AND FEEDBACK

- All grievances must first be raised with the Head of Department.
- If unresolved within 5 working days, the grievance may be escalated to the Principal's office.
- An anonymous feedback system is available via the faculty portal for reporting concerns without identifying oneself.
